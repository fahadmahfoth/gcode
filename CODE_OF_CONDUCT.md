# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this project:

- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements in a code review are about the code,
  not the person.
- **Critique the work, not the author.** "This regex is too broad" is useful.
  "This is careless" is not.
- **Accept a technical disagreement without escalating.** Disagreeing with a
  decision in an ADR is a normal part of the process, and the ADRs exist to make
  that discussion explicit.
- **Be patient with newcomers**, including people learning Rust and people whose
  first language is not English. Our user base is bilingual by design.
- **Credit contributors.** If someone's work is in the tree, their name or
  handle is in the history.
- **Accept AI-assisted contributions** at the same standard as any other
  contribution. See [docs/CONTRIBUTING.md](docs/CONTRIBUTING.md#ai-assisted-contributions).

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Special note on safety discussions

This is a tool that runs commands on people's machines. Discussions about
security, false positives in the risk classifier, and destructive commands are
**not** attacks on the project or on other contributors. A user reporting "gcode
classified this as SAFE and I lost data" has given us a bug report, and the
response is a fix.

Critique of the security design is not only welcome, it is the point. Critique of
the person presenting it is not.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
release or announcement channel — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private channel, not
a public issue. Use the contact on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/fahadmf).

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge the report
2. Review it, gathering context as needed
3. Decide on a response proportionate to the behaviour
4. Keep the reporter informed of the outcome

Responses, in escalating order:

| Response | When |
|---|---|
| A private correction | First minor offence, or a good-faith misunderstanding |
| A public warning | Repeated minor offences, or a warning after a correction |
| A temporary ban from project spaces | Serious or repeated violations |
| A permanent ban | Harassment, threats, or sustained violation |

Maintainers who do not enforce this code of conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent removal from the project.
